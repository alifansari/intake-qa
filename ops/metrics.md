## Definitions (so we don't move the goalposts)

- **Qualified conversation:** a decision-maker at a NorCal PI firm who has agreed to a call or
  reviewed a Leak Audit, not a cold reply.
- **Pilot:** a signed 30-day free engagement with a scoped Leak Audit deliverable.
- **Converted:** pilot → signed flat-fee monthly (Core or Pro).

## Review cadence

Weekly, at the top of the `/rocketship` loop. If an input metric hasn't moved in 2 cycles,
the research analyst investigates why before builders add more work on top.
