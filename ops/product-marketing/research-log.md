**Found (headline):** the entire pricing-power thesis holds — the buyer's default reference class

in all of Product Marketing is to break that reference class and reseat the product against a
*revenue-producing intake closer* (a $50–90k human) or, better, against *the fee on one signed
case the firm would otherwise lose.* Premium is a positioning outcome, not a price-table outcome.
