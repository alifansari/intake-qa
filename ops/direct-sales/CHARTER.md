## The metric this chat moves

Toward **$1M ARR in one year** (`ops/OPERATING-PROTOCOL.md`). This chat owns the sales-velocity
levers specifically:

- **Demos booked** (fill the calendar)
- **Demo → verbal-yes rate** (make the demo devastating)
- **Verbal-yes → signed rate + days-to-close** (compress the paper)
- **Signed → paid conversion** (the pilot doesn't leak)

Direct-sales north star: **cut demo-to-close to DAYS, not weeks**, at a repeatable win rate, for the
solo/small/mid PI segment. ~24 paying firms ≈ $1M, so this motion is the primary engine.

## The compliance envelope (non-negotiable — from compliance-invariants + the closer decision memo)

The product is an **English-language** AI voice agent. The demo-to-close motion lives INSIDE these
rails. They are a feature of the pitch, not a tax on it.

1. **The demo signs a MOCK lead.** A scripted simulation — no real caller, no dialing, no recording,
   no legal advice to a real person. That is a safe sales artifact. **Never demo, imply, or sell
   autonomous live-signing of real injured callers** as shipping behavior — the deployed product is
   **book-human-default qualifier + opt-in document-delivery e-sign** (see
   `ops/drafts/closer-book-human-default-decision-2026-07-12.md`). Sell the vision honestly; don't
   overstate the ship.
2. **No per-signed-case / outcome-tied pricing.** Rule 5.4 fee-splitting. Flat fee + optional
   included-minutes bucket only. Every pricing artifact must clear this (`compliance-invariants` §I).
3. **No unsupported claims (§IV — "no citation, no claim").** No "X% close lift," no "signs better
   than humans," no "only Spanish intake" until a measured/audited basis exists. Sell the mechanism
   and the *cost of the leak*, not an unproven outcome number.
4. **Consent-first, inbound-only, AI self-disclosure, no legal advice, human escape hatch** are the
   product's defaults and are part of the trust pitch (they DISARM the ethics objection — lead with
   them).
5. **English only.** The product and the demo are English-language. Do not build Spanish/bilingual
   into the motion, the demo, or any claim.

1. Read this charter + the three local ledgers + the shared brain.
2. Check `ops/backlog.md` and this folder's `backlog.md` for anything new Ali or other chats added.
3. Pull the highest-leverage open item; do the deepest possible research/build on it.
4. Ship the internal deliverable into this folder; update `playbook.md` if it changes the motion.
5. Log a dated entry in `research-log.md` and (if material) `ops/decisions.md`.
6. Re-score the backlog. If everything is genuinely exhausted, say so plainly and propose the next
   frontier rather than manufacturing busywork.
