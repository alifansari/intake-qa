## 0. The motion in one paragraph  ⏳
The fastest possible path from a cold PI firm to a paid, live agent: book a demo off a firm-specific
leak signal → run the mock-lead-signing demo → build the ROI number live with the lawyer's own
inputs → close on the call or within days on pre-staged paper → go live in 48–72h. Everything below
serves that.

## 2. The demo — the hero

**Devastating vs forgettable.** A voice-AI demo lives or dies in the first ~800ms; under that the
agent feels human, past it silence reads as incompetence and skepticism hardens ([SignalWire](https://signalwire.com/blogs/industry/what-latency-means-voice-ai)).
The forgettable demo is a feature tour over a slide; the devastating one is a single unbroken call
the buyer watches convert a stranger into a signature. Winners prove it — Eve sends the retainer
*during* the call ([LawNext](https://www.lawnext.com/2025/10/eve-launches-ai-intake-platform-with-voice-agent-for-plaintiffs-firms.html)),
Retell yields on barge-in in ~200ms ([Retell](https://www.retellai.com/blog/vapi-vs-bland)). Don't
describe the capability — trigger it live and go quiet. Live participatory demos can't be faked, so
they close harder ([Saleo](https://saleo.io/why-prospects-prefer-live-demos/)).

**Beat sheet (runnable, 5–7 min; rep talks <90 sec total):**
- **Beat 0 — Frame (20s):** "I'll place one call — a hurt person who found you at a bad hour. Watch
  *it*, not me. At one point *you* hand it a curveball. Everything you'll hear is a scripted
  simulation: no real caller, no dialing, no recording. Ready."
- **Beat 1 — Live call opens (45s):** distressed caller (rear-ended, ER, scared about bills). Agent
  **discloses it's AI in the first breath** (CA SB 1001), then leads with empathy before questions:
  "First — are you safe right now? I'm sorry this happened." Let the silence land — empathy-first is
  exactly where receptionists used to beat AI, so clear that bar first.
- **Beat 2 — Qualify live (60s):** mechanism → injury → treatment → fault → timeline; adapts tone,
  no robotic re-asking. The lawyer hears their own intake script run flawlessly at 2am.
- **Beat 3 — The curveball / anti-canned beat (45s):** cue the lawyer — "give it a fact that should
  DISQUALIFY this caller." They invent it (SOL 4 years ago / wrong state); the agent catches it live,
  declines to sign, books a human review. Catching a disqualifier the buyer invented in real time is
  where "that's canned" dies.
- **Beat 4 — Emotion/objection (30s):** caller wavers ("I need to think, I'm still shaken"); agent
  validates, offers a booked callback, holds the relationship. Restraint, not a bulldoze — partners
  fear a bot that steamrolls a vulnerable caller.
- **Beat 5 — Drive to signature → WOW (30s):** on a clean qualifier the agent triggers the e-sign
  retainer, audible and confirmed ("I've sent your retainer to the phone you're on — I'll wait while
  you open it"). The signature ping / booked-callback confirmation is the peak. Stop talking.
- **Beat 6 — The reveal (60s):** flip to the structured handoff — transcript, qualified/disqualified
  verdict with the verbatim quote, case type, deadline flag, dropped into the firm's queue. "That
  whole call became this, on your desk, with no one awake. That was 2am, and it still worked."

**Wow order (each raises the ceiling for the next):** 1) zero dead air → 2) empathy on a distressed
caller → 3) natural barge-in (interrupt it yourself) → 4) catching the invented disqualifier →
5) the audible signature → 6) "this was 2am and it still worked" (reframes it all as revenue they
sleep through).

**Kill "that's canned":** let them pick the scenario; run it on *their* practice areas; use the
Beat-3 disqualifier *they* invent. Show real proof (live latency, barge-in on demand, transcript→
handoff). Never invent a conversion % for their firm or claim the mock call was a real client;
borrow published outcomes WITH attribution (Eve's 10%→35% is *someone else's* firm — say so).

**<30-min personalization:** pull their practice areas from their site; script the mock caller to
match; find ONE real leak signal (after-hours voicemail, "we'll call you back" form, slow-callback
reviews) and open on it: "Your site sends after-hours callers to voicemail — here's the call they'd
have gotten instead."

**Honesty guardrail — exact rep language at Beat 6 (do NOT skip):**
> "Straight with you: what you watched is a scripted simulation — no real caller, no dialing, no
> recording. It shows the full range of what the agent can do. What we deploy on day one is set to
> **book a human callback by default**, and — only when you switch it on — send an e-sign retainer
> the caller opts into. The live signing you saw is the direction we're building toward, and *you*
> control how far down that road your firm goes. Nothing signs a real caller without you flipping
> that switch, and a human stays in the loop until you decide otherwise."

That admission is not weakness — to a Rule-7.1 buyer it's proof you won't lie to his clients either.
