## 2. GAPS WE CAN TRUTHFULLY EXPLOIT

1. **Structural independence.** Supio's Scoring Agent grades intake inside a platform whose own Voice Agent answers calls and whose business monetizes the downstream case (demands, chronologies, litigation). The grader, the graded, and the beneficiary of "sign more cases" sit in one vendor [verified structure: supio.com product pages]. We sell nothing else the score could flatter: flat fee, no voice agent, no demand product. Same argument as Eve's Auditor-grades-Jenny — state it as structure, never as accusation of bias.
2. **Enterprise motion vs. our beachhead.** Demo-gated pricing, $200–300K-OTE AEs, marquee mass-tort logos — the visible motion is mid-market-and-up [verified]. A 1–5 attorney CA firm gets a transparent flat price from us ($1,500/$3,000, founding $1,000) with no procurement cycle.
3. **SOP-compliance vs. case-underwriting.** Their published unit of grading is *your* SOP/script. Ours is a calibrated catalog of value-determining and disqualifying questions (UM/UIM, commercial defendant, priors, Prop 213), tiering the case and citing every finding to a transcript span [verified: their copy vs. our engine]. If the firm's SOP is weak, an SOP-compliance grade is a well-scored weak script.
4. **No visible develop-queue/SLA loop.** Report cards diagnose; nothing public shows a tracked worklist of unresolved case-making questions on an SLA clock [inference from absence]. That's our hero feature — lead with it.
5. **Bundle gravity.** Intake is the on-ramp to an end-to-end platform; deep adoption couples the firm's front door and case file to one vendor [verified structure]. We sit lightweight on top of existing phone/CMS with near-zero switching cost.
6. **California specialization.** Nothing public from Supio on CIPA/§632 consent workflow or CA-specific compliance framing [inference from absence]; it's our home turf.

## 4. TALK TRACK — "Isn't this what Supio does?"

- **Q: "Supio already scores every call."** A: "It does — as part of a platform that also answers your calls and builds your cases. We do one job: an independent flat-fee review, and we sell nothing the score could flatter. Different job: they're the intake operation; we're the audit of whoever runs it — including tools like theirs."
- **Q: "Why not one vendor for everything?"** A: "Fair choice for some firms. Ask any bundled vendor one question: what does the report card gain by finding problems in its own stack? Our only product is the finding, cited to the transcript."
- **Q: "Their report cards sound like your scorecards."** A: "Their published grading is against your intake SOP — did the script get followed. We grade whether the case-making questions got answered — UM/UIM, commercial defendant, priors — tier the case, and put every unresolved question in a 48-hour callback queue. The queue is the product; the score is the engine."
- **Q: "What do they cost vs. you?"** A: "They don't publish pricing — it's a custom quote after a demo. We publish ours: flat monthly, $1,500 Core, no per-case component, nothing tied to outcomes, ever."
- **Q: "We might adopt Supio's Voice Agent later."** A: "Great — keep us. We audit whoever answers, human or AI. If an AI takes your front door, independent verification that it didn't drop a viable case matters more, not less."
