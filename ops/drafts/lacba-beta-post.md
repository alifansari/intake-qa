**Beta testers wanted — 5 California plaintiff PI firms to help shape an intake-call review tool (free, NDA)**

A California legal-tech founder (Plaintiff Ops LLC) is taking five plaintiff personal-injury
firms into the founding beta of **Intake QA** — an independent review of your own recorded
intake calls that flags the signable cases that slipped through, so your staff can follow up
before that caller hires someone else.

He's looking for PI firms with steady inbound call volume that record their intake calls (or
are ready to start, with proper notice). The scoring is calibrated for PI intake today; other
plaintiff-contingency practices who'd like to help extend it are welcome to reach out.

What it asks of you: sign a mutual NDA (your calls and the beta stay confidential both ways),
share a sample of your recorded intake calls, and give honest feedback on whether the flagged
cases are genuinely signable and whether the tool is useful — that feedback is the whole point.

Free during the beta. Two things never change: your own staff make every callback (we never
contact your callers), and pricing at launch is a flat monthly fee, never a share of any recovery.

Start with a free review of 10 of your own calls at **plaintiffops.com/apply**, or email Ali at
**ali@plaintiffops.com**.
