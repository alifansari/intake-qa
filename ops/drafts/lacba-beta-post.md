## The post (~210 words)

**Beta testers wanted: 5 plaintiff firms for an intake-call review service (free, NDA)**

A California legal-tech founder (Plaintiff Ops LLC) is taking five firms into the founding
beta of **Intake QA** — an independent review of your recorded intake calls that flags the
signable cases your intake let slip, so your own staff can call them back before they hire
someone else.

**Who we're looking for:** plaintiff-side contingency firms — personal injury first (our
scoring rubric is PI-calibrated), but employment, and other plaintiff practices
are welcome to apply as we extend it. You should get meaningful inbound call volume and
record your intake calls (or be willing to start, with proper notice).

**What the beta asks of you:** a mutual NDA (both directions — your calls and our beta stay
confidential; BAA available); share your recorded intake calls; about 15 minutes a day
acting on what we flag; and honest feedback on the user experience and utility — that
feedback is the point of the beta.

**The terms:** free for 90 days. Two things never change: your staff make every callback —
we never contact your callers — and pricing at launch is a flat monthly fee, never a share
of any recovery.

Start with a free review of 10 of your own calls: **plaintiffops.com/apply**, or email
Ali at **ali@plaintiffops.com**.
