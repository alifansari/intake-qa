## ASSET A — The public-signal cold-open (B-011): "I looked at your public intake. Want the full autopsy?"

**What B-011 is.** The opener that earns the first reply by handing the partner ONE true, specific
observation about their *public front door* (what any prospective client sees), then offering the
full **10-Call Autopsy** on their own recorded calls. Two rails make it defensible:

- **CIPA-safe recon (§II).** Touch 0 observes only public, non-recorded signals. Ali calls the
  main line and listens to the IVR the way any caller would, submits the public web form, notes
  after-hours behavior, checks for a Local Services Ad badge. **He records nothing, and never runs
  a scripted fake-claimant scenario on a live intake human** (that is the separate, Yang-signed
  benchmark protocol, not this). The public front door is fair game; a person's live call is not.
- **No signal, no claim (§IV).** If recon surfaces no sharp, specific gap, Ali uses the
  argument-led variant (A-3) and invents nothing. Every opener must fail this test: *could this
  exact sentence be about any other firm?* If yes, it is not specific enough to send.

**The 6-signal recon checklist (~15 min/firm, public only):**

| # | Signal | How to read it (public only) | Routes to |
|---|--------|------------------------------|-----------|
| 1 | Spanish coverage | IVR offer Spanish? Site has a Spanish page but the phone tree is English-only? | A-1 (Spanish gap) |
| 2 | Web-form auto-response | Submit the public form; time any auto-reply. None / hours later? | A-2 (silence gap) |
| 3 | After-hours coverage | Call after hours: live service or generic voicemail? | A-2 |
| 4 | Speed-to-contact promise | Site promises "24/7" the front door does not keep? | A-2 |
| 5 | Paid-lead posture | Visible Google Local Services Ad badge = they pay per lead → sharpens the stakes | any (raises the dollar cost of a leaked call) |
| 6 | Intake ownership | Small/owner-operated? → "you feel every leaked call" framing | DM / voicemail |

**The cold-open one-liner (the reusable spine):**
`I did [specific public action] and noticed [exact observation]. That is the kind of gap that quietly sends a signable case home. Want the full autopsy on ten of your own calls?`

> **Compliance note (Asset A):** §II records nothing, tests only the public front door ✔.
> §IV no signal → no claim; A-3 fallback never fabricates a finding ✔. §III firm-directed to the
> managing partner, 1:1 ✔. §V no superlative, no guarantee ✔. §I no fee/outcome language ✔. The
> recon itself sends nothing — it only produces the opening sentence for Assets B/D.

I spent a year at a plaintiff-PI intake desk before I built anything.

Here is what that year taught me. The difference between a signed case and a lost one was almost
never the facts of the injury. It was the first four minutes of a phone call that no one in the firm
ever listened to again.

Think about what a personal-injury firm actually measures about intake. Cost per lead. Speed to
contact. Answer rate. Source. Sign rate by staffer. Every one of those describes the outcome of a
conversation. Not one of them describes the conversation. Whether the person on the line felt heard

the form. Whether a frightened caller left the call with their sense of control restored or stripped.

A grievance becomes a case only if it gets named, blamed, and claimed. Intake is exactly where that
transformation succeeds or fails, and it fails quietly, in language, on a recording no one plays back.

The other side of your cases figured this out a generation ago. Adjusters study the claim
conversation as a craft. The plaintiff side, the side that says it fights for the person, almost
never listens to its own tapes.

I think that is worth fixing, and I think it should be fixed by someone with nothing else to sell
you. Not the tool that answers your phones. Not the platform that profits from the case downstream.
An outside reader, against a published standard, who publishes their own error rate before asking you
to trust a single number.

That is the whole idea. Score the conversation, not just the outcome. Then hand back a worklist, not
a lecture.

If you run a California PI firm and this lands, I would value the conversation.

**Sequence rules (compliance-bound):**

- **A reply pauses the sequence.** A human (Ali) takes it from there; the cadence never talks over a

- **Every touch is Ali, personally, by hand.** No sending tool, no automation, no scheduled
  auto-send. This is the §VII human-in-the-loop chokepoint applied to outreach.
- **Business-day timing;** email lands mid-morning Tue–Thu; skip weekends. ~10–15 firms/day max.
