## 1. Who we are selecting (the strategic frame)

We need ~24 paying firms to reach the $1M exit run-rate; the market is ~800–1,500 serviceable
NorCal solo/small PI firms (insight C1, ESTIMATE). So **the constraint is not finding firms — it
is founder/analyst hours per closed firm** (insight B1). The Dream-25 doctrine follows: pick 25
firms worth a personal, physical, multi-touch campaign, and work them deeply rather than blasting
200. This document builds a candidate pool of ~60–80 firms, scores them with the rubric in §3,
and takes the top 25.

Geography is a hard filter: **Northern California only** — Greater Bay Area (SF, Oakland-Alameda,
San Mateo, Contra Costa), San Jose-Santa Clara, and Sacramento, with Fresno/Central Valley edge
as a secondary ring (insight C2). **No Southern California firms, ever** (standing targeting rule).

## 2. Ethical sourcing method (public sources only — how to BUILD the list)

Every source below is public and firm-directed (we research businesses, not claimants — §III/§VI).
No scraping of anything behind a login, no claimant data, no purchased consumer lists. The goal is
a firm-level pipeline record: firm name, managing partner name, office address, practice-area
confirmation, and the fit signals in §3 — all from public professional/business sources.

1. **California State Bar (apps.calbar.ca.gov).** The primary spine. Public attorney records
   confirm active status, admission date, and office location. Use to (a) verify a firm's
   attorneys are active and in good standing, (b) size the firm (attorney count), (c) confirm
   NorCal office location. This is the authoritative, public, ethical base layer. It does NOT
   tag practice area or firm size directly, so it is a *verifier*, not a discovery tool.

2. **County Trial Lawyers Association membership (public rosters/sponsor pages).** SF Trial
   Lawyers Association (SFTLA), Santa Clara County TLA, Capitol City Trial Lawyers (Sacramento),
   Alameda-Contra Costa TLA. Membership is a strong plaintiff-side signal and a NorCal-locality
   signal. Public member directories and event sponsor lists are the discovery layer.

3. **Consumer Attorneys of California (CAOC) NorCal chapters (caoc.org).** CAOC is the plaintiff
   bar's political home (it sponsored BOTH SB 37 and AB 931 — insight C2, VERIFIED). Its NorCal
   chapter rosters, board listings, and event sponsors are concentrated ideal-fit discovery AND
   the earned-authority distribution channel for the forthcoming benchmark. Public listings only.

4. **Google Local Services Ads (LSA) + Google/PPC ad presence.** Firms showing the LSA
   "Google Screened" badge or running paid PI search ads are — by definition — **paying per lead**,
   which means leakage has a hard dollar cost to them (the exact pain the Leak Audit addresses).
   Ad presence is public. This is the single sharpest *fit* signal in the whole method (rubric §3,
   signal 3). Google Ads Transparency Center shows which advertisers run ads.

5. **Avvo / Justia / Super Lawyers NorCal PI directories.** Practice-area confirmation and
   partner names at scale. These are directory listings, not a census (insight C1 caveat), so use
   them to *discover* candidates and *confirm practice area*, then verify everything against the
   State Bar (source 1). Never treat a directory count as ground truth.

**Cross-verification rule:** a firm enters the pipeline only when confirmed by ≥2 independent
public sources (e.g., LSA ad presence + State Bar active status + directory practice-area tag).
One source is a lead; two is a candidate. This is "no citation, no claim" (§IV) applied to
list-building.

## 3. The "ideal-fit" scoring rubric

Score each candidate 0–3 on each signal; sum for a fit score (max 18). Take the top 25 into the
Dream-25. Signals operationalize insight C3.

| # | Signal | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | Why it matters |
|---|--------|---|---|---|---|---------------|
| 1 | **Size (2–10 attorneys)** | Solo w/ no intake staff, or >15 attys | 11–15 | Solo w/ intake team, or 8–10 | **2–7 attys** | Big enough to have paid lead flow + an intake team; small enough the owner *feels* every leaked case and decides fast (C3). |
| 2 | **PI case mix (volume PI)** | Not PI | Mixed, PI minor | PI-heavy | **Auto/MVA + premises volume PI** | High intake volume + $300–$1,500 lead cost = leakage has real dollars (C3, B4). |
| 3 | **Pays per lead (ad-spend behavior)** | No visible paid acquisition | Some SEO only | Running PPC/LSA | **LSA "Screened" badge + active PPC/TV** | They pay per lead, so a leaked call is money already spent and lost — the sharpest pain signal (C3). |
| 4 | **Intake-pain signals** | Fast human answer, bilingual, modern | — | Generic answering service or slow web follow-up | **After-hours→voicemail, English-only phone tree on a Spanish landing page** | Direct evidence of the leak we sell against; the Spanish gap doubles as the PR wedge (C3, C4). |
| 5 | **Owner psychographic** | Unknown / not growth-facing | — | Some metrics/marketing signal | **Growth-minded, metrics-curious, publicly "cases we let slip"-aware** | The audit lands only with an owner who feels the pain and will act on a number (C3). |
| 6 | **NorCal locality (hard gate)** | Outside NorCal → **DISQUALIFY** | Central Valley edge | Sacramento / San Jose | **Core Bay Area** | Targeting rule: NorCal only, no SoCal ever (C2). Signal 6 = 0 removes the firm entirely regardless of total. |

**Selection rule:** disqualify on Signal 6 = 0 (not NorCal) OR Signal 2 = 0 (not PI), regardless
of total. Among the rest, rank by total fit score; ties broken by Signal 3 (pays-per-lead), since
demonstrable per-lead spend is the clearest sign the ROI story lands.

## 4. What this plan explicitly does NOT do

- **Does not fabricate firms.** No invented names, partners, addresses, or phone numbers appear
  here or in any downstream draft. Real targets come only from the §2 public sources, cross-verified.
- **Does not touch claimant data.** Everything is firm/business-level and public (§III/§VI). We
  never research or list an injured person.
- **Does not authorize contact.** A finished Dream-25 list is an input to the batch-1 outreach
  (which is itself staged, not sent). Ali approves both the list and each send (§VII).
- **Does not scrape logged-in or paywalled data**, and does not buy consumer/lead lists.
