## Method (how each firm was verified)

- **Discovery:** public directory + search sweeps across the target metros — Greater Bay Area
  (SF, Oakland/East Bay, Contra Costa), San Jose–Santa Clara, Sacramento, plus the
  Sonoma and Central Valley secondary rings. Sources: firm websites, Justia/Avvo/Super Lawyers,
  Google Business/Yelp business listings, and the **California State Bar licensee search**
  (apps.calbar.ca.gov) as the authoritative active-status/location spine.
- **≥2-source rule (per targeting plan §2):** every firm below was confirmed by at least two
  independent public sources — typically (a) the firm's own website AND (b) a State Bar
  licensee record and/or an independent directory (Justia/Super Lawyers) confirming the name
  partner is active and NorCal-located. One source = a lead; two = a candidate.
- **Fit score:** the §3 rubric, 0–3 on six signals (max 18). Disqualify if Signal 6 (NorCal) = 0
  or Signal 2 (PI) = 0. Ad-spend/lead-gen (Signal 3) and intake-pain (Signal 4) are often only
  partially observable from outside, so those sub-scores are **estimates** and labeled as the
  softest part of each score.

## Date-independent caveats (read before using)

- **Addresses, partners, phone numbers drift.** Everything here is a snapshot from public pages
  as of the research date. **Re-verify each mailing address against the State Bar record + the
  firm site immediately before any physical mailer** — the mailer is the strongest touch and a
  wrong address wastes it.
- **Attorney counts are approximate.** Most firm sites don't publish a headcount; where a number
  appears it's a count of attorneys *listed on the team page*, which can lag reality. Treated as
  a range, not a fact.
- **Signal 3/4 (ad-spend, intake-pain) are largely inferred.** We did not run a paid mystery-shop
  (that needs the CIPA §632 protocol + Yang sign-off, `compliance-invariants` §II — still staged).
  So the leak/pain signals are external inferences, not measured. This is the honest soft spot.
- **No personal emails were guessed.** Per the task rule, partner emails appear only if the firm
  publicly publishes one; otherwise "not public — use intake form / office phone."

## HONEST data-quality count

- **15 firms** on the list, all with name partner + city + mailing address + website +
  plaintiff-PI focus verified from ≥2 public sources.
- **"Unverified / needs check" fields:** attorney **size** is the weakest column — **9 of 15**
  firms do not publish a headcount, so those sizes are estimates from partner/team-page counts,
  not confirmed. **1 firm** (Phoong Law) had **no mailing address on the cited practice page** —
  the address shown is taken from its State Bar/Justia record and must be re-confirmed.
- **Personal partner email: 0 of 15** are publicly published in a safe-to-use form — all default
  to the firm intake page or listed office phone. This is expected and correct (§III/§VI).
- **Ad-spend/intake-pain (Signal 3/4):** directly verified for only **1** firm (Phoong, public
  reporting of a ~$5M ad budget); for the other 14 it is inferred from SEO/PPC footprint and is
  the least certain input to the score.
