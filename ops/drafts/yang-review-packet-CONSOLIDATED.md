## COVER — read this first (one page)

**What Intake QA is (one paragraph).** A flat-monthly, outcome-agnostic quality-control and
internal-follow-up vendor to plaintiff PI law firms. We ingest the firm's *own* recorded intake
calls (their consent chain), score them against a fixed rubric, and surface the signable cases their
reps let leak — so the *firm's own staff* call back the *firm's own* prior inbound callers. We
recommend, refer, secure, and solicit *no claimant for any lawyer*; we never share in a recovery.
The fee is a flat monthly subscription, invariant to whether the firm signs zero cases or fifty. Ali
is "Analyst of Record," an independent scorer — **not a fee participant** (compliance §I).

**What launches Monday 2026-07-14.** A *free* beta with a founding cohort: pricing removed, the $25k
guarantee suspended, checkout disabled, SMS/texting dark, staff-callback only. No public dollar
figure ships. The live `/compliance` page argues only from the §§6151–6152 runner/capper framework
and a flat-fee framing — it does **not** yet claim "AB 931-compliant" or "SB 37-compliant."

**The single most time-sensitive decision.** **Item 1 — bless the exact §I fee-structure replacement
text (Table C) + the founding-MOU "12-month lock / steps to $2,500" sentence.** It is the master
unblock: until the §I language is settled, CR-B (pilot→paid conversion, the weakest number in the
whole model) is literally un-observable, the Charter offer cannot go live, and three staged docs keep
quoting three different price tables. The *number* is Ali's call; the *fee-structure wording* is
novel-in-a-regulated-area and routes to you. Everything downstream inherits whichever table its
author happened to read.

**The one hard Monday blocker.** **Item 4 — the contracts packet.** `/apply` promises firms "your NDA
within one business day; nothing connects until it is signed," yet the NDA is not yet Yang-cleared,
carries a "zero-retention" overclaim in §4(c), and there is no signable DPA to hand counsel. A
one-business-day clock over an uncleared document is a broken promise waiting to happen on day one.
