# Intake QA — Engine v2 Delta & Open-Questions Summary

**Scope.** This is the "what do we actually change" document for the existing scoring‑v2 package (CITED‑fact extraction + 7 anchored dimensions from the LLM; four gates, a four‑way decision table, value/effort/carry/capital tiers, volume‑vs‑selective posture, and observability‑derived confidence/abstention in deterministic code). Nothing below asks to re‑derive the architecture. Every item is a diff against extraction schema, a dimension anchor, a gate trigger, a decision‑table row, a tier lookup, the posture config, the prior library, or the confidence/abstention layer.

**Compliance rails are preserved throughout:** tiers not dollars; urgency FLAGS not computed dates; lawyer ratifies every disposition (nothing auto‑declines); fairness (unobserved ≠ negative, lay‑vocabulary severity parity, deferential/apologetic speech ≠ fault admission, per‑language disparate‑impact audit); "no citation, no claim"; abstain rather than guess.

**Provenance discipline.** Statutory/numeric constants are marked **[config]** (year/date‑indexed, operator‑visible, not flywheel‑overwritable). Published economics and base rates are marked **[prior — published, not this firm's]** and decay under the firm's own resolved‑case data. Corrections applied by the corpus verifiers are folded in and flagged where they overturn a researcher framing.

## 1. Prioritized diff list (ranked by triage‑accuracy impact ÷ effort)

### Tier A — Ship first: highest accuracy lift, low/medium effort, mostly config + schema

**A1. Re‑baseline every statutory constant to date‑indexed config lookups.** Several anchors are keyed to obsolete numbers; this is the single cheapest accuracy win because it corrects the *value math itself*, not a judgment call.

| Constant | Current/obsolete | Correct 2026 value | Keying rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Auto minimum liability limits (SB 1107) | 15/30/5 | **30/60/15** | Applies to policies **issued/renewed on/after 1/1/2025**, *not* loss date. Prefer stated limits; use policy/loss date only as fallback. Further step to **50/100/25 on 1/1/2035**. |
| MICRA non‑economic cap (AB 35) | flat $250k | **$470k injury / $650k death (2026)** | Indexed to **year of RESOLUTION**, not intake year. A 2026 intake resolves ~2027–2029 → use the **projected resolution‑year** cap ($510k/$700k in 2027, etc.). Schedule: +$40k/+$50k per yr to $750k/$1M in 2033, then +2%/yr. |
| Limited‑jurisdiction ceiling (AB 2347) | $25k | **$35k** (eff. 1/1/2024) | MIST/small‑value tier boundary. |
| Rideshare UM/UIM (SB 371) | $1M | **$60k/$300k** (eff. 1/1/2026) | See A5 — branch on at‑fault party and app period. |

Sources: [SB 1107 text](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=202120220SB1107); [Nolo MICRA 2026](https://www.nolo.com/legal-encyclopedia/how-does-the-micra-damage-cap-affect-california-medical-malpractice-case.html); [Milliman AB 35](https://us.milliman.com/en/insight/how-will-ab-35-affect-micra-and-non-economic-damage-caps).

**Verifier correction folded in:** MICRA is **not a single hard case ceiling**. It caps *non‑economic only* (economic uncapped) and **stacks up to three category caps** (providers collectively / institutions / unaffiliated), so a hospital‑plus‑physician case can carry ~$1.41M (injury) / ~$1.95M (death) in non‑economic exposure at 2026 caps. Justify med‑mal refer‑out on **capital/expert intensity**, never on a $470k "ceiling," or the engine will systematically under‑tier high‑economic‑damage med‑mal. Add extraction `defendant_is_institution` vs `defendant_is_individual_provider` to drive stacking.

**A2. Promote `defendant_type` to a required first‑class extraction field.** This is the largest single value multiplier and the strongest recurring competitor signal (EvenUp flags it explicitly; ~20× settlement swing on identical injury). Enum: `individual / commercial / rideshare / trucking / government / uninsured / unknown`, each with cited quote span. Wire as the **primary anchor in collectability/deep‑pocket** and a primary input to the **value tier**; `government` additionally fires the urgency flag in A4. Lexical anchors for casual mentions ("the bus said Metro on it", "company truck", "Uber"). Sources: [EvenUp AI Playbooks](https://www.evenuplaw.com/blog/ai-playbooks-voice-agent/); [Sweat CA settlement data](https://www.victimslawyer.com/blog/average-personal-injury-settlement-in-california-2026-real-data-by-injury-type-severity-and-insurer/).

**A3. Split the coverage stack into distinct cited facts, and make client‑side UM/UIM the decisive fallback.** Add `def_BI_limits`, `client_UM_UIM` (present/absent/limits), `medpay_PIP`, `umbrella`, `multi_policy`. Two structural rules:
- **G1 override:** adequate client UM/UIM downgrades an otherwise‑underwater case from DECLINE toward **DEVELOP** (recovery path exists through the client's own carrier). Decision‑table branch: valid liability + real injury + no defendant coverage + UM present → **DEVELOP** (UM claim); no UM either → **DECLINE‑WITH‑GRACE**.
- **Non‑stacking + offset:** total realistic coverage = `max(defendant_limit, client_UIM)` minus offset, **never the sum** (Ins. Code 11580.2). Client‑side coverage comes straight from the client's own dec page (immediately obtainable); defendant BI limits are *not* reliably obtainable pre‑suit (see A9 / DEVELOP). Sources: [Ins. Code 11580.2](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=INS&sectionNum=11580.2.); [Plaintiff Mag UM/UIM](https://plaintiffmagazine.com/recent-issues/item/the-abc-s-of-um-uim-cases).

**A4. Public‑entity defendant → tightest urgency FLAG + G2 escalation.** Add `public_entity_defendant` (bool), derived from a checklist: govt/marked vehicle (bus, police, fire, city, school district), injury on public property (sidewalk/road/park/public building), public hospital/clinic, public employee in scope. When true: fire the **6‑month government‑claim** flag (Gov. Code 911.2) and route to G2 malpractice‑trap. Add sub‑flags: `entity_sent_rejection_letter` (Gov. Code 945.6 restarts a 6‑month suit clock; 2 years if no compliant rejection) and — **verifier correction** — `claimant_is_minor` (Gov. Code 911.4/911.6 grant **mandatory late‑claim relief to ~1 year** for a minor, so a minor is *not* hard‑barred at 6 months). Dangerous‑condition‑of‑public‑property (Gov. Code 835) additionally requires the entity's own notice — cap liability at THIN without a notice fact. FLAGS only, never a computed date. Sources: [Gov. Code 911.2](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=GOV&sectionNum=911.2); [Gov. Code 835](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=835.&lawCode=GOV); [Advocate "Rule of Six"](https://www.advocatemagazine.com/article/2016-november/the-rule-of-six-when-suing-a-california-public-or-governmental-entity).

**A5. Rideshare must branch on at‑fault party and app period, not "rideshare = $1M."** Add `rideshare_period` (`off / on_waiting / enroute_or_passenger / unknown`) and `at_fault_party` (`rideshare_driver / third_party / disputed`). Rules, date‑gated at crash date 1/1/2026:
- Rideshare driver at fault, Period 2–3 → **$1M** liability available.
- Injured passenger, *uninsured third party* at fault, passenger‑onboard window → coverage capped at **$60k/$300k** (SB 371, PUC 5433(b)(2)) — can drop a serious‑injury case a full value tier and trip G1.
- Period 1 (app on, no match) → thin contingent limits (~$50k/$100k/$30k with a $200k excess layer per PUC 5433(c)) → treat like a min‑limits case.
- Period unknown → **abstain / DEVELOP** ("which period?"), never tier on assumption. Sources: [J&Y Law SB 371/AB 1340](https://jnylaw.com/blog/california-cuts-rideshare-insurance-limits-and-gives-drivers-union-rights-what-that-means-after-an-accident/); [CA DOI rideshare periods](https://www.insurance.ca.gov/0400-news/0100-press-releases/archives/release067-15.cfm).

**A6. Objective‑findings + permanency as the damages‑credibility backbone (mirror Colossus).** Add `injury_objective_findings` (`none_subjective_only / imaging_finding / fracture / surgical / documented_neuro_deficit`), `permanency_indicator`, `surgery_performed_or_recommended`, `treatment_intensity`, `disfigurement_stated` (+region; face/hands weight higher), `functional_impairment_stated`. Anchor: THIN = subjective soft‑tissue, no corroboration; ADEQUATE = ≥1 demonstrable finding; STRONG = demonstrable finding **plus** permanency or surgery. Permanency is the #1 Colossus value driver; it multiplies the injury‑rung base tier. Because imaging/surgery/permanency are records‑based and rarely confirmed on a first call, a promising‑but‑unverified severe injury defaults to **ADEQUATE + DEVELOP**, not STRONG/SIGN‑NOW. Sources: [Nolo on Colossus](https://www.nolo.com/legal-encyclopedia/how-the-colossus-computer-program-estimates-accident-settlement-values.html); [CACI 3905A](https://www.justia.com/trials-litigation/docs/caci/3900/3905a/); [CACI 3903A](https://www.justia.com/trials-litigation/docs/caci/3900/3903a/).

**A7. Treatment‑latency and gap facts → damages‑credibility, fairness‑gated.** Add `days_incident_to_first_treatment`, `treatment_gap_over_30_days`, `gap_explained` (cited reason). First visit >~1 week or an unexplained >30‑day gap pushes the anchor toward THIN — *unless* a cited reason (delayed onset, ER‑then‑referral, financial/access barrier) neutralizes it. **Fairness gate:** on a fresh‑incident first call the gap is usually not yet observable → `unobserved ≠ negative`, abstain rather than penalize. Never route to G3. Sources: [Butler Firm treatment gaps](https://butlerfirm.com/blog/how-insurance-companies-use-gaps-in-medical-treatment-against-you/); [Advocate pre‑existing conditions](https://www.advocatemagazine.com/article/2015-april/how-plaintiff-s-pre-existing-conditions-affect-general-damages).

### Tier B — High impact, medium effort: gate refinements and the net‑recovery rework

**B1. Re‑specify G1 "Underwater" to fire on PROJECTED NET after statutory lien reductions, not gross bills.** This is the highest‑value gate change and the most under‑modeled term in net fee per attorney‑hour. Add a **lien‑load tier lookup** keyed by `(lien_type, statutory_reducibility_rank)`:

| Lien type | Reducibility rank | Statutory basis | Net effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Medi‑Cal | 1 — most reducible | Ahlborn medical‑only (WIC 14124.76) → −25% fees (WIC 14124.72(d)) → 50%‑of‑net cap (WIC 14124.78), "least‑of" | Large gross → modest net; **should not push G1** |
| Hospital Lien Act | 2 | 50%‑of‑net cap (Civ. 3045.4) + Howell/*Huff* reasonableness; only bites when hospital did **not** bill health insurer | Bounded |
| Workers'‑comp (3rd‑party) | 2–3 (fact‑contingent) | Witt v. Jackson employer‑fault credit; LC 3856/3860 | Reducible only via litigated employer fault |
| Medicare conditional payments | 3 | Procurement‑cost fee‑share (42 CFR 411.37); federal super‑lien | Passes through more than Medi‑Cal |
| **ERISA self‑funded** | **5 — least/non‑reducible** | Plan language trumps made‑whole + common‑fund (*US Airways v. McCutchen*) | **The disposition‑flipper** — can claw back ~100% |

Change the trigger from `gross_lien_tier ≥ limits_tier` to `projected_net_lien_tier ≥ limits_tier`. Add extraction: `client_insurance_status`, `government_benefit_status`, `on_the_job_injury`, `hospital_admission`, `treated_on_lien`, `lien_sources{...}`. **Critical abstention rule:** self‑funded‑vs‑insured ERISA status is almost never knowable on the call → set reducibility = `unknown` and route to **DEVELOP** ("obtain SPD/plan funding status"), never worst‑case guess and never auto‑DECLINE. Sources: [WIC 14124.78](https://codes.findlaw.com/ca/welfare-and-institutions-code/wic-sect-14124-78/); [Ahlborn](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/547/268/); [McCutchen](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/569/88/); [Civ. 3045.4](https://codes.findlaw.com/ca/civil-code/civ-sect-3045-4/).

**Do not add a liability‑MSA / future‑medicals carve‑out** — no LMSA is required in 2026 (CMS rule withdrawn again); reserve MSA logic strictly for the workers'‑comp branch. Source: [Sanderson Firm — LMSA withdrawn](https://www.sandersoncomp.com/blog/lmsawithdrawn).

**B2. Refine G1 Prop 213 sub‑trigger to a precise fact conjunction with carve‑out suppressors.** Add `claimant_own_vehicle_insured`, `claimant_role` (driver/owner/passenger), `defendant_DUI_convicted`, `accident_on_private_property`. Fire the non‑economic bar **only** when claimant was an uninsured **owner/operator** in an auto case AND no exception. **Verifier corrections:** (a) Civ. 3333.4 also bars a claimant convicted of DUI in the same crash and a felon injured during felony/flight — add `claimant_own_DUI`; (b) it does **not** apply to passengers — guard against false underwater flags for passengers; (c) the DUI‑*defendant* exception (subd. c) requires an actual **conviction** tied to the (a)(2) uninsured‑owner scenario and is usually unknown at intake → emit a *contingency flag* and route DEVELOP, not a firm bar; (d) statutory exceptions (employee in employer's uninsured vehicle, permissive user under another policy, wrongful‑death heirs, punitive, product‑liability) each suppress the bar for that damage stream. The bar reaches **non‑economic only** — economic damages survive, so a high‑medical case stays viable. Sources: [Civ. 3333.4](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=3333.4.&lawCode=CIV); [Advocate "Into the weeds of Prop 213"](https://www.advocatemagazine.com/article/2025-october/into-the-weeds-of-prop-213).

**B3. G4 trial‑capital gate + carry tier keyed to a track cost curve.** Seed a two‑dimensional cost‑advance lookup `(case_track × litigation_status)` **[prior]**: pre‑suit soft‑tissue auto <~$1k; same case in‑suit $5k–$20k; premises/litigated auto mid; med‑mal **$30k–$50k+ floor**; trucking/catastrophic **$150k–$300k+** expert exposure. Carry = `cost_advance × months_to_resolution` (pre‑suit ~9 mo, litigated ~24 mo). Expose a posture‑config `capital_ceiling` (~$100k default per the credit‑line cap); when open‑docket float approaches it, posture shifts selective. Fee rate is `litigation_status`‑dependent (0.33 pre‑suit / 0.40 post‑filing) so DEVELOP‑then‑litigate paths net the higher fee against higher cost. Sources: [CAALA cash‑flow](https://www.advocatemagazine.com/article/2021-november/managing-cash-flow); [med‑mal cost floor](https://www.helbocklaw.com/how-much-is-a-medical-malpractice-case-worth-in-california/); [trucking expert spend](https://www.blockotoole.com/truck-accidents/).

**B4. REFER‑OUT as first‑class, ROI‑positive disposition with a computed threshold.** Refer out when `in_house_net_fee_per_attorney_hour < referral_net_fee_per_attorney_hour`, where referral value ≈ 0.25–0.33 × expected net fee at ~0 firm hours/capital (pure referral) or 0.40–0.50 (active co‑counsel). CA uniquely permits **pure referral fees** (Rule 1.5.1: written client consent, no fee increase, no joint‑responsibility requirement). REFER‑OUT output attaches a standing Rule 1.5.1 compliance note (no dollar/fee figure). Add posture params `refer_up_threshold` (keyed to capital/capacity) and `referral_split_default`. High‑cost/high‑merit tracks (trucking, med‑mal, catastrophic) frequently compute a *higher* fee‑per‑hour as a referral than in‑house — the decision table must make this explicit, not treat referral as failure. Sources: [Rule 1.5.1 Exec Summary](https://www.calbar.ca.gov/sites/default/files/portals/0/documents/rules/Rule_1.5.1-Exec_Summary-Redline.pdf); [Feher Law referral guide](https://feherlawfirm.com/california-attorney-referral-fees-rule-1-5-1-guide/).

**B5. Rebuild Dimension 6 (client‑risk) as strictly behavioral, with hard exclusions.** Confirmed marker set (each needs a verbatim behavior quote): `fee_negotiation_attempt`, `repeated_value_inquiry` / fixation on a payout figure, `worth‑going‑to‑doctor` question, `disrespect_toward_staff`, self‑reported serial attorney‑shopping (fired 2+ prior lawyers *for fee reasons*). **Baked‑in prompt exclusions:** distress, crying, anger at the at‑fault party, accent, language, limited English, deferential/apologetic/self‑minimizing speech, demographics, "sounds unsophisticated" are **never** markers. G3 can only FLAG, never auto‑decline; `unobserved ≠ negative`. **Prior counsel existing is NOT a marker** — a lawful switch is a case fact routed to G2/conflict check, not G3. Add per‑language flag‑rate audit hook. Sources: [Champion Firm intake](https://www.thechampionfirm.com/blog/personal-injury-law-intake-improvements/); [CACI 405](https://www.justia.com/trials-litigation/docs/caci/400/405/).

**B6. Split the fault signal to neutralize apology/deference.** Add `factual_admission` (a concrete admitted act: "I looked at my phone", "I rear‑ended them") vs `politeness_affect` (apology, hedge, self‑blame with no factual basis). Liability/comparative‑fault dimension and G1/G2 may consume **only** `factual_admission`. Anchor Dimension 1 to CA **pure** comparative negligence (Li v. Yellow Cab): a fault percentage alone is *never* FATAL — FATAL requires unprovable liability *and* thin damages, or a complete legal bar. Log an audit counter on apology‑adjacent language to confirm no penalty path. Sources: [Cutter Law — pure comparative](https://cutterlaw.com/california-laws/comparative-negligence/); [CACI 405](https://www.justia.com/trials-litigation/docs/caci/400/405/).

### Tier C — New case‑type coverage (medium effort, closes real routing gaps)

**C1. Premises subtypes as distinct extraction paths.** Split into transitory‑hazard slip‑fall, sidewalk/walkway trip‑fall, negligent‑security, structural defect, public‑entity. Add `hazard_origin`, `last_inspection_interval_evidence` (absence of inspection records is an affirmative pro‑plaintiff signal — *Ortega*), `defect_height_estimate` + `aggravating_conditions[]` (trivial‑defect ~1.5 in. is a **soft prior with mandatory aggravator extraction, never an auto‑decline** — *Stathoulis*), `prior_similar_incidents_on_property` + `requested_security_measure_burden` (negligent security — *Ann M./Delgado*, keyed to property crime history only, never victim demographics). Guardrail: do **not** auto‑upgrade a self‑service retail slip‑fall to STRONG on venue alone (*Moore v. Wal‑Mart* — mode‑of‑operation does not waive notice). Sources: [Ortega](https://law.justia.com/cases/california/supreme-court/4th/26/1200.html); [Plaintiff Mag — trivial defect](https://plaintiffmagazine.com/recent-issues/item/get-your-sidewalk-fall-case-to-the-jury-beat-the-trivial-defect-defense); [Plaintiff Mag — third‑party criminal conduct](https://plaintiffmagazine.com/recent-issues/item/premises-liability-cases-involving-third-party-criminal-conduct).

**C2. Dog‑bite: liability threshold + collectability gate.** Add `injury_mechanism` (bite/knockdown/scratch — strict liability under Civ. 3342 requires an actual **bite** + lawful presence), `victim_location`, `owner_housing_status`, `bite_location_on_owner_premises`, `dog_breed`, `prior_aggression_history`, `landlord_actual_knowledge_evidence` (*Uccello/Yuzon* — landlord liable only on actual pre‑attack knowledge). Value‑tier seed prior ≈ CA average ~$80k **[prior]** — require cited aggravating‑damages facts to move above it. Collectability is the decisive signal: no homeowner/renter policy, off‑premises renter bite, or excluded breed → THIN → G1. Sources: [Civ. 3342](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=3342.&lawCode=CIV); [III dog‑bite data](https://www.iii.org/article/spotlight-on-dog-bite-liability).

**C3. Product liability defaults to REFER‑OUT, with a narrow keep exception.** Add `product_defect_theory` (manufacturing/design/warning), `product_category`, `consumer_expectation_fit`, `in_state_seller_in_chain`. Design‑defect/automotive → default REFER‑OUT + G4. Override to keep: `consumer_expectation_fit = yes` AND `in_state_seller_in_chain = present` (joint‑and‑several, Prop 51 does not limit strict‑liability recovery). Source: [Plaintiff Mag — small firm products](https://plaintiffmagazine.com/recent-issues/item/can-a-smaller-firm-handle-a-products-liability-case).

**C4. Med‑mal & government as their own SOL/urgency rows.** Med‑mal: CCP 340.5 (1‑yr discovery / 3‑yr outer, minor‑under‑6 to age 8), CCP 364 90‑day notice‑of‑intent (itself a trap on a near‑SOL case → refer to a specialist who can execute the mechanics). Add `date_of_treatment`, `date_discovered`, `patient_is_minor`, `notice_of_intent_served`, `provider_admitted_deviation` vs `known_complication_language` (do not let outcome severity inflate the liability read — severity‑parity rail; grade liability THIN/UNKNOWN on bad‑outcome‑only). Med‑mal default **REFER‑OUT** unless clear deviation **and** catastrophic economic damages. Sources: [CCP 340.5](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=340.5&lawCode=CCP); [CCP 364](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CCP&sectionNum=364).

**C5. Three currently‑unmodeled solo‑PI types.**
- **WC third‑party civil claim** (LC 3852): add `injury_on_the_job`, `third_party_defendant_identity`, `employer_fault_indicators` (Witt v. Jackson can shrink the comp lien → positive net modifier), and exclusive‑remedy exception flags (`power_press_guard_removed` LC 4558, `employer_manufactured_product`, `employer_uninsured`, `fraudulent_concealment`, `employer_assault`) that convert the employer into a civil defendant. Pure comp‑only, no third party, no exception → **REFER‑OUT** to a comp specialist, not DECLINE. Sources: [LC 3852](https://impactattorneys.com/labor-code-%C2%A7-3852-employers-right-to-reimbursement-from-third-party-recovery/); [Witt v. Jackson — Advocate](https://www.advocatemagazine.com/article/2019-march/workers-compensation-liens-and-credit-issues).
- **Elder/dependent‑adult abuse** (WIC 15657): add `victim_is_elder_or_dependent_adult`, `care_facility_defendant`, `recklessness_indicators` (cited), `victim_deceased`. Fee‑shifting is mandatory on clear‑and‑convincing recklessness → **value multiplier biasing toward SIGN/keep**, and *Delaney v. Baker* holds it is **not** MICRA‑capped. **15657(b) survives the CCP 377.34 sunset** — a deceased victim's pre‑death pain/suffering remains recoverable (capped only by Civ. 3333.2(b)), unlike ordinary survival actions. Fork Dimension 7: statutory elder abuse (high value, fee‑shift, MICRA‑free) vs ordinary nursing‑home negligence (MICRA‑capped, no fee‑shift) — abstain to DEVELOP if the transcript can't distinguish recklessness from negligence. Sources: [WIC 15657](https://codes.findlaw.com/ca/welfare-and-institutions-code/wic-sect-15657/); [Delaney v. Baker](https://law.justia.com/cases/california/supreme-court/4th/20/23.html).
- **Wrongful death / survival standing** (CCP 377.60/377.30/377.34): add `claimant_relationship_to_decedent`, `claimant_financial_dependency`, `claim_posture` (WD‑only / survival‑only / both), `known_additional_heirs`. Post‑1/1/2026 **survival damages revert to economic‑only** (SB 447 sunset) **except** elder abuse — a survival‑only intake with no living 377.60 heirs and no elder‑abuse recklessness is materially lower value. Never sign assuming the caller is the sole heir (one‑action rule) → DEVELOP "confirm/join all heirs." Sources: [CCP 377.60](https://codes.findlaw.com/ca/code-of-civil-procedure/ccp-sect-377-60/); [CCP 377.34](https://codes.findlaw.com/ca/code-of-civil-procedure/ccp-sect-377-34/).

**C6. Trucking spoliation + settled broker liability.** Add `vehicle_is_commercial_truck`, `cargo_type`, `employer_carrier_identified`, `broker_or_shipper_mentioned`, `commercial_truck_evidence_present`. Fire a **CRITICAL** urgency flag `spoliation‑letter‑needed` (ELD/HOS ~6‑mo, in‑cab dashcam 1–3 days) whenever `vehicle_is_commercial_truck` → biases viable case to SIGN‑NOW/immediate‑action and in‑house over refer (delay risks spoliation), bounded by G4. FMCSA coverage floors ($750k general / $5M hazmat, 49 CFR 387.9; passenger 387.33) set the coverage‑path floor. **Verifier correction — supersedes Miller/Gallagher‑Sharp:** *Montgomery v. Caribe Transport II* (SCOTUS, 14 May 2026) unanimously holds the FAAAA safety exception preserves negligent‑selection claims against freight brokers nationwide — broker liability is now a **settled collectability factor**, not a contested upside flag. Sources: [49 CFR Part 387](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/subtitle-B/chapter-III/subchapter-B/part-387); [FMCSA ELD](https://eld.fmcsa.dot.gov/).

### Tier D — Adds precision where value/collectability is currently over‑ or under‑stated

**D1. Howell/Corenbaum/Pebley/Audish payment‑posture coupling.** Add `coverage_treatment_path` (`health_insurance_in_network / lien_or_out_of_network / uninsured / unknown`) and `claimant_medicare_status`. Insured plaintiff → billed charges are an **upper bound only** (Howell/Corenbaum); grade on paid basis. Uninsured/lien plaintiff → billed/reasonable value admissible (Pebley) → higher gross, but feed the lien into G1/carry (nets down). Future medicals for a Medicare‑eligible/near‑65 plaintiff → apply an **Audish discount** (defense may prove future care at Medicare rates). Tie‑break: for future medical, Audish reliability‑discount dominates the Pebley uplift when Medicare eligibility is cited. **Verifier correction:** Howell record is ~$189,979 billed / ~$59,692 paid (fix any seeded example that used $174k/$41k). Sources: [Howell](https://law.justia.com/cases/california/supreme-court/2011/s179115/); [Audish — Lewis Brisbois](https://lewisbrisbois.com/newsroom/legal-alerts/calif.-appeals-court-permits-use-of-medicare-reimbursement-rates).

**D2. Prior‑claims / same‑body‑part credibility (route to Dimension 2, NOT G3).** Add `prior_injury_or_claim_same_body_region`. **Verifier/premise correction — DROP any "run ISO ClaimSearch" develop‑action:** ISO discloses to insurers only, not plaintiff counsel. Pre‑suit action is a **structured client interview** (5‑yr treatment history + all prior auto/WC claims); the defense ISO report is obtainable only post‑filing via RFP. At phone intake a records contradiction is usually not observable → anchor `unknown` pending records, don't penalize. Add `active_or_undisclosed_bankruptcy` → FLAG for attorney review (estate‑ownership / judicial estoppel), never auto‑decline. Source: [ISO limits](https://cpollardlaw.com/personal-injury-attorney-virginia/iso-claimsearch-past-accidents/).

**D3. DUI/malice — separate the reliable liability signal from the contingent punitive value.** Add `defendant_intoxicated_or_DUI` (observed_convicted / charged / alleged / unknown), `aggravating_conduct_facts` (extreme speed, ran signal, flight, erratic — *Taylor/Dawes* conscious disregard), `defendant_is_individual_vs_entity`, `personal_asset_signal`, `punitive_target_beyond_driver` (dram‑shop/employer/entity). Rules:
- DUI/intoxication weights **heavily and unconditionally into liability** (near‑zero comparative fault).
- Punitive VALUE is credited **only** when `aggravating_conduct_facts` present AND collectability supports it (entity, asset signal, or deep‑pocket target) — because **punitive damages are uninsurable in CA** (Ins. Code 533 / *City Products*), an award against a bare‑min‑limits individual is uncollectable. Against such a defendant, emit an "punitive‑upside, collectability‑unverified" review FLAG, never a firm value bump.
- Because conviction/asset status is usually unknown at intake and CA gates financial‑condition discovery (Civ. 3295), punitive value is **DEVELOP‑stage**; abstain rather than self‑assert. Sources: [Taylor v. Superior Court](https://scocal.stanford.edu/opinion/taylor-v-superior-court-28126); [Ins. Code 533](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=533&lawCode=INS); [Civ. 3295](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=3295.&lawCode=CIV).

**D4. Lay↔clinical severity lexicon + TBI/radiculopathy proxies (fairness parity).** Add `loss_of_consciousness`, `cognitive_symptoms_stated`, `imaging_present`, `neuro_deficit_stated`. Decode lay phrases to clinical rungs **before** grading: "my hand goes numb, can't grip" ≡ radiculopathy; "I blacked out / I'm not myself" ≡ TBI rung. Anchor language: grade severity from described symptoms and functional impact, **not vocabulary sophistication**; lay phrasing at a symptom level scores the same as clinical phrasing; never downgrade for absence of medical terminology. (Attorney‑reviewed phrase bank + per‑language audit required before ship — see §5.)

## 3. Methodology upgrades worth adopting

All harden the deterministic/observability layer around v2; none require abandoning the architecture. **Architecture‑transfer caveat:** the strongest sources (SCOPE, position‑bias, language‑bias) are *pairwise* A‑vs‑B judging; v2 does *absolute anchored* grading. The **principles** transfer; the specific mechanisms (bidirectional preference entropy) are pairwise artifacts to re‑adapt. **Domain‑transfer caveat:** every headline number is from math/QA/chat benchmarks, not PI‑intake — treat all as directional priors to re‑measure on attorney gold.

1. **Conformal risk control for abstention (M‑high).** Replace the heuristic confidence threshold with distribution‑free finite‑sample risk control: reserve ~40–50% of attorney‑labeled gold as a conformal calibration split, define a nonconformity score from observability signals, solve for the largest threshold meeting a chosen α (e.g. 5–10% max wrong among "auto‑ratifiable"). Implement via the **absolute‑setting formulation** ([DeepMind conformal abstention, 2405.01563](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.01563)), not the pairwise SCOPE guarantee. Recalibrate on every model/prompt change.
2. **Multi‑signal abstention fusion (M‑high).** Entropy/self‑report alone is provably insufficient; fuse: (a) **order/paraphrase‑invariance disagreement** (grade twice with permuted anchor/fact order at temp 0), (b) **generative‑verifier disagreement** (a prompted pass that reads only cited facts + candidate disposition and answers "justified?"), (c) **citation‑grounding failure count**, (d) **primary‑vs‑canary model divergence**, (e) per‑fact extraction confidence. Feed the vector into the conformal threshold.
3. **Enforce "no citation, no claim" mechanically (M‑high).** For each of the ~30 cited facts: string/fuzzy‑match the quote span against the transcript, then a lightweight NLI/entailment check that the span supports the value+speaker. On failure, drop the fact or force `unknown` — a fabricated quote must never trip G1/G2 or inflate damages. Use small‑NLI faithfulness detectors (MiniCheck/FactCG class); the legal‑domain [Citation Grounding, 2606.00898](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15240) is closer than CiteCheck.
4. **Retire same‑model temp‑0 self‑ensembling; buy reasoning effort or model‑diverse ensembling instead (M‑med).** Self‑consistency j=1→7 gave no accuracy gain at temp 0; reallocate to higher reasoning effort and a **Prometheus‑2‑class open‑weight canary** grading the same 7 rubrics — its divergence is both a drift alarm and the cheapest true model‑diverse signal.
5. **Never derive confidence from reasoning length / self‑rated certainty (M‑high).** Longer CoT reliably worsens calibration. Codify as a hard rail; add an audit that dimension LEVEL is uncorrelated with cite‑then‑claim reasoning length.
6. **Translation‑invariance parity audit (M‑high, fairness).** Upgrade the per‑language audit from histogram comparison to a causal test: grade held‑out calls in source Spanish AND English translation; systematic ES‑lower deltas at equal audio quality are a **release blocker**. Grade on **English‑normalized extracted facts** (extraction stays source‑language for verbatim citation) so surface language can't proxy severity. Note: Spanish is high‑resource, so expect a *small* delta — measure, don't assume.
7. **Gold‑set construction (M‑high).** Dawid‑Skene reliability‑weighted aggregation (not majority vote) for multi‑attorney labels; report **QWK with bootstrap CIs + Krippendorff's α**; gate model promotion and drift alarms on the **CI lower bound**, not the point estimate.
8. **ASR is the dominant fairness threat, not the grader (M‑high).** Add per‑fact `asr_segment_quality` (SNR, detected‑language prob, code‑switch density, hallucination/insertion flag). A low‑quality or silence/non‑speech‑mapped segment forces the citing dimension to `unknown` and routes DEVELOP — audio quality can only widen abstention, never justify an adverse level. `detected_language` is **never** an abstention input; only measured audio quality and code‑switch ratio are. Extend "no citation" to "no clean audio, no citation." Sources: [Koenecke ASR disparity](https://www.pnas.org/doi/pdf/10.1073/pnas.1915768117); [ASR overconfidence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.07195).
9. **Four‑fifths disparate‑impact audit as a monitor (M‑med).** By detected language and interpreter‑mediated vs not, track favorable‑disposition rate, abstention rate, confidence, DECLINE rate; flag any group <0.80 of the top for **human review** (never auto‑adjust thresholds; never a language‑correlated DECLINE). Treat 0.80 as **necessary‑not‑sufficient** and always run a significance test (small solo‑firm cells need Fisher/Bayesian shrinkage). Do not imply legal safe‑harbor — this is an internal monitor, not a Title‑VII obligation. Source: [29 CFR 1607.4(D)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disparate_impact).

## 5. Execution ceiling — what research cannot improve

Research can supply the *structure* (fields, anchors, gate logic, statutory constants, published priors, methodology). It **cannot** supply the following; these require real transcripts and attorney‑ratified labels:

1. **The strong/adequate/thin boundaries themselves.** Anchors are calibrated ordinally from sources; the actual cut points that match "how CA PI attorneys want it" can only be fit against attorney‑labeled calls. Until then the grader should widen its abstention band on freshly‑seeded dimensions.
2. **Every value‑tier dollar band except the statutory anchors.** No free CA‑specific *settlement* (vs verdict) distribution by case type or county exists — verdict reporters are paywalled and tried‑case‑biased; MIST/soft‑tissue bands are marketing aggregators. True value tiers require the firm's own resolved‑case flywheel or a licensed verdict feed. `value_tier` and `dog_rate` are the first two fields the flywheel overwrites.
3. **Hours‑per‑case — the objective‑function denominator.** No public source gives attorney/paralegal hours by track for a 1–5‑attorney firm. Effort must be proxied off track + litigation‑status and corrected only from the firm's own timekeeping instrumentation.
4. **Base‑rate priors (dog_rate, settle_prob, liability‑win) are national/insurer/verdict‑biased.** CA settlement reality sits between BJS verdict medians and IRC paid means; only firm outcomes resolve the true correction factor.
5. **Whether each conditional field is actually observable on‑call.** Rideshare period, at‑fault party, client UM/UIM limit, GVW/cargo, self‑funded ERISA status, DUI conviction, defendant assets — the *base rate at which each is stated on a first call* (vs must route to DEVELOP) can only be measured on real transcripts, and it sets how often the engine can move off ABSTAIN.
6. **ASR error and language‑parity magnitudes on your audio.** The published overconfidence/hallucination/insertion numbers are Whisper‑ and benchmark‑specific; the observability thresholds must be calibrated to your production model on your own Spanish + code‑switched CA‑PI audio.
7. **Fairness *accuracy* parity vs disposition‑rate parity.** Without attorney‑ratified outcomes labeled by language, the audit can only show equal *rates*, not equal *correctness* — it cannot distinguish "fair" from "equally wrong across languages." That distinction requires a labeling plan.
8. **The lay↔clinical severity lexicon and DEVELOP‑action dimension‑lift weights** need attorney review and outcome backtesting, respectively — a phrase bank shipped without an attorney‑reviewed, per‑language disparate‑impact pass would itself introduce a penalty path the rails forbid.

In short: **research got the engine to a defensible, rail‑compliant v2.1 spec; only real transcripts + attorney labels can turn its anchors, tiers, priors, and abstention thresholds from calibrated‑from‑literature into calibrated‑to‑this‑firm.**
