I am not going to quote you the tired secret-shopper statistics every intake vendor waves around;

Let me measure you. Then argue with the number.

## ASSET D — The 3-touch sequence plan (mail → email → LinkedIn)

*Concentrated, personal, multi-touch on ONE firm — the Dream-25 doctrine. Attention is scarce;
each touch is a different channel so the firm meets the same voice three ways.*

| Touch | Day | Channel | Asset | Purpose |
|-------|-----|---------|-------|---------|
| 1 | Day 0 | Physical dimensional mail | Asset A (signed letter + Leak Audit one-sheet) | The object that gets remembered. Lead with authority + the free wedge. |
| 2 | Day 4–6 | 1:1 email | Asset B | "I sent you a letter" — bridges the physical touch, gives an easy reply path. Timed so the letter has landed but not gone cold. |
| 3 | Day 8–12 | LinkedIn | Asset C (connect, then opener on accept) | Third channel, same voice. Humanizes the sender; lets a partner who ignores mail/email engage on their turf. |

**Sequence rules (compliance-bound):**
- **Hard stop at 3 touches** per firm in this batch. No fourth touch without a new decision. No
  drip-forever cadence — that is exactly the blast behavior §III forbids in spirit.
- **Any opt-out at any touch ends all touches for that firm, immediately and permanently** — an
  email "no thanks," a LinkedIn "not interested," a returned letter marked refuse. Log it.
- **A reply pauses the sequence** — a human (Ali) takes it from there; the cadence never talks
  over a live conversation.
- **Every touch is Ali, personally.** No sending tool, no automation, no scheduled auto-send.
  This is the human-in-the-loop chokepoint (§VII) applied to outreach.
- Touch timing is business-day based; skip weekends so email lands mid-morning midweek.

> **Compliance note (Asset D):** §III non-blast, capped, opt-out-honoring, firm-directed ✔.
> §VII every send is a manual human action by Ali — no autonomous cadence ✔. Reply/opt-out both
> halt the sequence. No SMS, no autodialer, no pre-recorded anything ✔.
