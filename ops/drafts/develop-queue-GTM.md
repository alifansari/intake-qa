## 1. Positioning
**One-liner:** "An independent recovery desk that reviews your OWN reps' recorded intake
calls, tells you which case-making questions got asked vs missed, and runs a tracked
queue chasing the unresolved ones until they're answered — so high-value cases don't slip
out before you ever see them."

**3-bullet value prop (survives a skeptical partner):** (1) **your reps' calls go un-QA'd
today** — everyone reviews signed cases, almost nobody reviews the intake *conversation*
that decided whether a case got signed (the blind spot your CRM/case managers structurally
can't cover — they only see cases that made it through); (2) **we resolve the questions,
not just flag them** — the scorecard is the diagnosis, the **develop-queue** (unresolved
value-determining/disqualifying questions, each on an SLA clock) is the treatment and the
money; (3) **flat fee, independent, no skin in your fees** — not a fee participant, lead
vendor, or contingent; one predictable number, which is *why* the score is worth anything.

**Anti-surveillance framing (or staff sabotage it — per intake-staff research):** never
"we grade your people" → **"we catch the cases before they're lost and hand your reps the
callback list that makes them look good."** Scores are **desk-level by default** (unit =
the case that almost leaked, not "Maria missed 4 questions"); per-rep rollups OFF by
default, partner-gated. The queue is a **to-do list handed TO the rep** ("6 callbacks
worth making this week" = help), not a report card ABOUT them. Rep is the hero of the
recovery. **Develop-queue is the HERO, QA-scoring is the engine** — lead every
deck/demo/email with the queue + the SLA metric; competitors can score calls, almost
nobody runs a tracked SLA'd remediation queue (the defensible primitive).

## 2. The demo that closes — the "Almost-Lost Case card" (run on the firm's OWN calls)
Not a deck. Ingest 2–3 weeks of the firm's own recorded calls (CIPA-safe consent chain),
hand-pick ONE genuine near-miss (read as high-value tier, rep missed 2–3
value-determining/disqualifying questions). Live script (compliance baked in — tier +
estimate-with-assumptions, NEVER a promised dollar recovery; deadlines generic; every
claim cites a transcript span):

> "Before we talk about us, here's one call from *your* desk — Tuesday 2:14pm. Your rep
> did a lot right [ALWAYS open with what the rep did well — protects staff, disarms the
> partner]: got the mechanism, confirmed insurance. But three questions didn't get asked:
> a possible **second at-fault commercial vehicle** ('the truck behind him' at 3:47, never
> followed up); prior treatment/gap-in-care (decides whether it survives a defense IME);
> and whether they'd already called another firm (a disqualifier). On the mechanism + the
> commercial-vehicle hint we tiered this **high-value — IF that commercial-policy question
> pans out** (an estimate with assumptions, not a promise). Right now it's sitting in your
> CRM as a routine sign-up with none of those three answers. Our system put those three in
> your **develop-queue with a 48-hour SLA** — that's the product: not 'your rep messed
> up,' a callback list that turns a maybe into a high-tier case. This is one of [N] we
> found in three weeks. Want to see the other [N-1]?"

Why it closes: their data, their dollars, their Tuesday → reframes spend from "cost" to
"the case I almost lost pays for a year," and shows the queue doing what no dashboard does.

## 4. Beta → paid motion (the 5-firm beta = a case-study factory + proof engine)
(a) **Generate 2–3 "you almost lost this" cards** — the analyst's explicit job is to find
the single best Almost-Lost Case per firm in the first 3 weeks (anonymized, cited, value
as tier+assumptions) → conversion artifact + (permissioned) de-identified reference.
(b) **Prove the SLA→recovered-dollars correlation** — instrument from day one: questions
surfaced, % resolved within SLA, cases that changed tier after a queued question was
answered. Earn the claim "firms resolving >X% within SLA upgraded N cases/mo" —
confidence-tiered, directional, NEVER a guarantee (weak at N=5; don't let it harden).
(c) **Convert** — the conversion meeting IS the §2 demo on their own beta data + their
instrumented numbers; trigger = **end of a FIXED window (~6 weeks)** + a founding-price
cliff (open-ended free betas never convert). Agreement: flat monthly fee (no outcome-tied
language anywhere), short term, clear scope, CIPA consent chain + deletion cascade on
offboard, Ali as **Analyst of Record / independent scorer — NOT a fee participant**, no
recovery guarantee. Novel fee/consent wording → Yang (§VII); agent drafts + stages, a
human sends.

## 6. Objection table
- **"My intake director already does this"** → your director reviews the cases that got
  SIGNED; we review the conversations incl. the calls that DIDN'T convert (never
  re-listened to), and we run a tracked SLA'd callback queue — your director gets a
  prioritized list, not more work.
- **"My CRM has QA"** → the CRM stores the call; it doesn't tell you which value-determining
  question was skipped, tier the case, or chase the answer on a clock — and CRM QA fields
  are filled by the same person who took the call (self-grading). We're the independent
  scorer + the queue is the part the CRM structurally doesn't do.
- **"AI will upset my staff"** → we don't grade individuals by default; reps get a callback
  list that makes them look good, not an error report; scores are desk-level, the case is
  the unit. They adopt it because it hands them wins.
- **"How is this not CallRail/Observe.AI?"** → those are call TOOLS (recording, keywords,
  agent-efficiency dashboards, sold by the seat to ops managers); we're an independent
  recovery desk sold to YOU on cases you almost lost; they stop at the transcript, we run
  the SLA'd queue that resolves the misses. Different buyer, job, price. (Never lead with
  "we transcribe calls" — they do too.)
- **"What's it cost?"** → flat monthly, no share of fees, nothing tied to outcomes, ever;
  Core $1,500 (founding locks $1,000/yr); one recovered high-tier case pays for a year
  several times over, but you pay the same flat number whether we find one or ten.
- **"Will this get me a bar complaint?"** → the opposite — built by the compliance line:
  flat fee never a cut of recovery (the §6152/Rule 5.4 line), independent scorer not a fee
  participant or lead vendor, value TIERS never a promised dollar, every finding cites the
  transcript, methodology reviewed by a former Deputy Chief Trial Counsel of the State Bar.

**Two §VIII flags:** (1) the beta correlation claim is only as strong as N=5 — present
confidence-tiered/directional, never a guarantee. (2) the demo's tier/estimate is the
highest compliance-risk moment — keep it tier + estimate-with-assumptions, never a promised
recovery, and have Yang bless the exact demo-script language once before first live use (§VII).
