## 1. The one thing this plan maximizes

**Qualified conversations booked per founder-hour, toward a 3–5-firm founding cohort.** Not reach,
not impressions, not applications. The binding constraint is **founder-hours per qualified
conversation** (insight B1: we need ~24 paying firms out of an ~800–1,500-firm serviceable
universe — 2–3% penetration; the cohort is only 3–5). At that target, one warm intro that converts
at 30–50% beats a 4,000-reach listserv post that converts at ~0 compliantly. So the whole plan
concentrates hours where trust is highest and the compliance surface is cleanest, and lights the
slow-compounding channels *now* so they pay off before the warm supply runs out.

**Qualified conversation** (held to the `metrics.md` definition): a decision-maker at a CA PI firm
who agreed to a call or reviewed a Leak Audit. A cold reply is not one.

Two engines, deliberately. The warm/1:1 engine produces the founding cohort but **cannot scale to
24 paying firms** — warm supply exhausts in ~2–3 weeks and 1:1 is founder-hour-bound. The scalable
channels have multi-week-to-month lead times, so they must be *lit this week* even though they book
nothing this week. Running only the near-term engine is the plan's biggest structural risk (§6).

## 5. THE MONDAY CHANNEL, named — and why

**Start Monday with: warm intros (row 1) + the 1:1 email/LinkedIn autopsy sequence (row 2), fired
together, under a CAALA-affiliate warm frame.** This is the only combination that can book qualified
conversations *the week the beta opens* while staying dead-clean on §III/§V. Dream-12 mail primes it;
CAALA affiliate ($400) makes every touch read "fellow member"; the LinkedIn carousel + Advocate pitch
are air cover started the same week.

**Why this and not "get in front of 4,000 on the LACBA listserv":** the cohort is 3–5 firms, not
3,000. At that target the constraint is hours, not reach. A worked warm intro converts at ~30–50%; a
listserv post converts at **~0 compliantly** — because the compliant action is *not to post*. LACBA's
published Listserve Guidelines bar commercial content, and a beta-recruitment post for a paid product
is commercial **even when free during the beta**. Handing the member drafted copy is the proxy dodge
the `publication-channel-brief` already rejected for the article version; free-during-beta does not
launder it.

**The single highest-leverage move of the week — convert, don't post.** The LACBA Small Firms member
who volunteered to blast 4,000 attorneys is worth far more as an **introducer** than as a billboard.
The reply to him: *"Rather than put you in an awkward spot with the section's commercial-use rule, the
biggest help would be a warm intro to 2–3 firms you think actually have this problem."* That is his
speech plus a personal vouch (row-1 economics, ~30–50% not ~0), it can't blow back on the bar or the
brand, and it re-points the single best relationship we have this week from a channel that can only
hurt us to one that can only help. **Re-head `lacba-beta-post.md` with this no-listserv ruling** so no
future session resurrects it.

## 6. The biggest risk (named, not smoothed — §VIII)

**The near-term engine is structurally supply-capped and founder-hour-bound.** Warm intros + 1:1 can
plausibly book the 3–5 founding firms, but cannot reach the ~24 paying firms the $1M yardstick
requires: warm supply runs out in ~2–3 weeks, and 1:1 is bounded by Ali's hours. If the
slow-compounding authority channels (CAALA events, the independent benchmark, Advocate/podcast) are
**not started this week**, the funnel stalls in month 2–3 with nothing scalable warmed up behind it.
Mitigation is baked into §4: light the authority engine on Day 0 even though it books nothing on Day 0.

**Secondary risks:** (a) the two decisive rates — warm-intro conversion (~30–50%) and touch→qualified
(~8%) — are both **plausible-unverified estimates**; if real rates come in low, the whole per-hour
ranking compresses, which is why instrumenting touch→qualified from firm #1 is mandatory, not optional.
(b) **autopsy→paid** is unproven and load-bearing for the entire $1M timeline (see `autopsy-wedge-playbook.md`
§9). (c) **The pricing gate is still BLOCKED ON ALI** — three staged docs disagree on the price table;
until Ali writes ONE table into compliance-invariants §I, every asset here quotes **no number**, which
keeps them send-safe today but means the first real close needs the number locked (decisions.md 2026-07-10).
