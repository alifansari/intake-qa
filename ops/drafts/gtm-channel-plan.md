## The one question this answers

**Which channels return the most _qualified conversations per founder-hour_** for a solo,
pre-revenue founder selling an *independent* flat-fee intake-QA desk to CA plaintiff-PI firms,
with the beta opening Monday 2026-07-14 and a target of **3–5 founding firms**?

"Qualified conversation" (metrics.md definition, held to): a decision-maker at a CA PI firm who
**agreed to a call or reviewed a Leak Audit** — not a cold reply, not an impression, not a like.

**The binding constraint is not reach and not TAM** (insights B1: we need ~24 paying firms out of
an ~800–1,500-firm serviceable universe — 2–3% penetration). The constraint is **founder-hours
per qualified conversation.** So every channel below is scored on that axis, not on audience size.
A channel that reaches 4,000 attorneys but converts none of them per hour of your work loses to a
channel that reaches 3 the right way.

---

## How the qual-conv/hr estimates were built (so you can attack the assumptions)

These are **ESTIMATES**, arithmetic exact, conversion rates assumed. The two rates that decide
everything (per insights B2): **touch→qualified** and, downstream, pilot→paid. I anchor
touch→qualified to the funnel model's **~8% on warm, personalized, non-blast outreach** (insights
B2, labeled there as an estimate), then adjust up for warmth (intros) or down for cold/one-way
(mail-only, cold LinkedIn content). Where a channel is calendar-gated (events) or has a multi-week
lag (mail, editorial, podcasts), I say so — a high per-hour rate that only fires in September does
not help the Monday cohort.

Every external number is tagged **[verified]** (primary source + URL) or **[plausible-unverified]**.

## The LACBA member-proxy gray zone — resolved honestly (and against us)

**The situation:** a LACBA Small Firms member *volunteered* (email 2026-07-10) to post a beta-team
request to 4,000+ LA-area attorneys, and asked us for the specifics. We drafted the post
(`lacba-beta-post.md`). It is tempting: free, huge, warm-ish reach the day the beta opens.

**Why it's still a dodge — the honest read:** LACBA's published Listserve Guidelines restrict the
listserv to "official business of the group," not "commercial purposes... or to advertise or market
non-LACBA related programs" [verified, lacba.org/?pg=Listserve-Guidelines, per publication-channel-brief].
A post recruiting customers for a paid SaaS product is commercial content **even when the product
is free during the beta** — the recruitment builds a paying pipeline. The `publication-channel-brief`
already ruled on the *article* version: "A member posting the branded piece at our request is proxy
commercial use of a channel that bans commercial use; if traced back, it burns the member and brands
us as the vendor who gamed the bar's listserv." **The beta post is the same act with a different
noun.** That we didn't ask him and he offered does not launder it *if we hand him ad copy to paste* —
scripting the post is the orchestration the rule reaches.

**The line, stated cleanly:**
- **His unprompted speech is his.** If he independently writes, in his own words, "I met a founder
  doing interesting independent intake-QA work; he's looking for a few beta firms — DM me if
  curious," that is his editorial choice. We may *tell him the beta exists*. We may **not** draft the
  post, supply the copy, or request the placement.
- **The higher-value, unambiguously-clean move is to convert the offer.** A member willing to vouch to
  4,000 peers is worth far more as an **introducer** than as a billboard. Member-to-member
  introductions are not listserv commercial posts, they carry a personal vouch (row-1 economics:
  30–50% not 8%), and they can't blow back on the bar or on us. So the reply to him becomes:
  *"Rather than put you in an awkward spot with the section's commercial-use rule, the biggest help
  would be a warm intro to 2–3 firms you think actually have this problem."*

**Net:** row 7's 4,000-reach is a mirage at the compliant conversion rate; the *same relationship*
re-pointed at row 1 is arguably the single highest-yield asset we have this week. Do not treat this
as a loss — treat it as a redirect from a channel that can only hurt us to one that can only help.

**Also re-check:** `lacba-beta-post.md` should be re-headed with this ruling so no future session
resurrects it as a listserv post. (Flag, not a live edit.)

## Recommended Monday-week channel(s)

**Primary: Warm intros (row 1) + the 1:1 email/LinkedIn sequence (row 2), fired together, with
CAALA affiliate joined as the warm frame under both.** This is the only combination that can book
qualified conversations *the week the beta opens* while staying dead-clean on §III/§V.

Concretely, the Monday-week motion:
1. **Tonight/Mon AM (row 1):** write the 5-name intro list; send 3 personalized intro asks. **Reply to
   the LACBA member converting his listserv offer into 2–3 named introductions** (the gray-zone
   resolution above — this is the single highest-leverage hour of the week).
2. **Mon–Fri (row 2):** run the `dream25-outreach-batch-1.md` sequence on the first 5 Dream-12 firms;
   free Leak Audit is the wedge in every touch; log **reply-by-channel and touch→qualified from firm
   #1** (insights B2: instrument the two rates that decide the whole model).
3. **This week, low-cost enablers:** join CAALA Affiliate ($400 — Ali's call) so every touch reads
   "fellow CAALA affiliate," and mail the **Dream-12 signed letters (row 4)** as touch-1 primers.
4. **After the Yang read:** publish the five-questions piece as a **LinkedIn carousel (row 5)** for air
   cover; send the **Advocate pitch (row 6)** already staged in `publication-channel-brief.md`.
5. **Never this week:** the listserv post (row 7) and the podcast circuit (row 8).

**Why this and not "get in front of 4,000 on the listserv":** the founding cohort is **3–5 firms**,
not 3,000. At that target, one warm intro that converts at 30–50% is worth more than a 4,000-reach
post that (a) converts at ~0 compliantly and (b) carries tail risk to the member and the brand. We
are hours-constrained, not reach-constrained (insights B1). Concentrate the hours where trust is
highest and the compliance surface is cleanest.

**Honest caveat baked in:** rows 1–2 will produce the founding cohort but **will not scale to 24
paying firms** — warm supply runs out and 1:1 is founder-hour-bound. The channels that scale (CAALA
events, the independent benchmark as an earned-authority engine, the Advocate/podcast authority
stack) have **multi-week-to-multi-month lead times** and must be *started now* to pay off in months
2–4. Start the slow authority engines this week even though they book nothing this week; harvest the
warm/1:1 engine for the Monday cohort.
