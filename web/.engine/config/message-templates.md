ATTORNEY-APPROVED SMS TEMPLATES (firm-editable — safe to edit)

These are the ONLY starting points allowed for re-engagement drafts. A drafting
model personalizes them per caller but must NOT break the constraints below.
Edit the wording to fit your firm's voice; keep every rule intact.

CONSTRAINTS (baked in — enforced in code, do not remove):
  * Identify the firm by name.
  * Warm, empathetic, human tone.
  * NO legal advice, case evaluations, or opinions on the merits.
  * NO pressure, urgency tactics, or salesy language.
  * The FIRST message MUST include opt-out language ("Reply STOP to opt out").
  * Under 320 characters total.
  * Placeholders: {{firm_name}} and {{first_name}} are filled at draft time.

COMPLIANCE NOTE: California inquiry-based EBR consent is tight (~30 days; best
practice is same-day re-engagement). These drafts NEVER send on their own — a
human approves every message first.
